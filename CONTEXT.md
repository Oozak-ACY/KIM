# KIM (Keep In Mind)

A mobile memory companion that resurfaces short pieces of knowledge, captured from AI conversations or entered by hand, at the moment they are about to be forgotten. KIM reminds, it does not teach.

## Language

### Cards

**Card**:
A short, self-contained piece of knowledge the user wants to keep in mind, written to be re-read in seconds.
_Avoid_: Flashcard, note, concept, memory card

**Cue**:
The subject of a Card phrased as a short question or title, used to trigger recall.
_Avoid_: Title, prompt, front

**Essence**:
The key knowledge of a Card, capped at about three bullets or 280 characters, and never rewritten by KIM.
_Avoid_: Answer, back, content, summary

**Hint**:
An optional mnemonic, analogy or example attached to a Card.
_Avoid_: Tip, note

**Source**:
The assistant and conversation a Card was captured from.
_Avoid_: Origin, reference

**Tag**:
A label grouping related Cards, suggested at Capture.
_Avoid_: Deck, folder, category

### Reviewing

**Review**:
One short session where a due Card is shown again to reactivate the memory, lasting 10 seconds to 3 minutes.
_Avoid_: Lesson, study session, reminder

**Recall mode**:
The default Review style: the Cue is shown, the user tries to remember, reveals the Essence, then says whether they remembered.
_Avoid_: Post-it mode, read mode

**Quiz mode**:
An optional per-Card Review style where the user answers a question about the Card before the reveal, then grades themselves as Forgot, Hard or Good.
_Avoid_: Test, exam

**Nudge**:
The push notification that invites the user to a Review, phrased as a question about the Card's Cue.
_Avoid_: Alert, reminder notification

**Nudge window**:
A user-chosen time range during which Nudges may be sent, subject to a daily cap.
_Avoid_: Schedule, slot

### Capture

**Capture**:
The act of creating Cards from an AI assistant conversation through the KIM connector, after the user confirms the drafts in the conversation.
_Avoid_: Import, sync, export
