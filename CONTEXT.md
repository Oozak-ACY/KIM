# KIM (Keep In Mind)

A mobile memory companion that resurfaces short pieces of knowledge, captured from AI conversations or entered by hand, at the moment they are about to be forgotten. KIM reminds, it does not teach.

## Language

French UI terms: Card is **Carte**, Cue is **Repère**, Essence is **Essentiel**, Tag stays **Tag**, Nudge stays **Nudge**, Nudge window is **Plage de Nudges**. _Indice_ stays free for Hint.

### Cards

**Card**:
A short, self-contained piece of knowledge the user wants to keep in mind, written to be re-read in seconds.
_Avoid_: Flashcard, note, concept, memory card

**Cue**:
The subject of a Card phrased as a short question or title, used to trigger recall.
_Avoid_: Title, prompt, front

**Essence**:
The key knowledge of a Card, capped at about three bullets or 280 characters, and never rewritten by KIM.
_Avoid_: Answer, back, content, summary

**Hint**:
An optional mnemonic, analogy or example attached to a Card.
_Avoid_: Tip, note

**Source**:
The assistant and conversation a Card was captured from.
_Avoid_: Origin, reference

**Quiz question**:
A question with its expected answer attached to a Card, written by the assistant at Capture or by the user, rotated across Reviews in Quiz mode.
_Avoid_: Variant, exercise

**Archived**:
A Card that no longer gets Nudges, still kept and searchable, either by the user's choice or after accepting KIM's proposal once the Card was remembered at a six month Review.
_Avoid_: Deleted, hidden, paused, mastered, acquired

**Tag**:
A label grouping related Cards, added by the user or suggested at Capture.
_Avoid_: Deck, folder, category

### Accounts

**Account**:
Who owns a set of Cards. Every user starts with an anonymous Account on their device, and becomes **linked** once they sign in with Google or an email link, which keeps their Cards and syncs them across devices.
_Avoid_: Profile, user (for the Account itself)

**Linked**:
An Account that has signed in with Google or email at least once, as opposed to an anonymous one. Connecting an assistant and receiving Nudges require it.
_Avoid_: Registered, signed up, verified

### Reviewing

**Review**:
One short session where a due Card is shown again to reactivate the memory, lasting 10 seconds to 3 minutes.
_Avoid_: Lesson, study session, reminder

**Recall mode**:
The default Review style: the Cue is shown, the user tries to remember, reveals the Essence, then says whether they remembered.
_Avoid_: Post-it mode, read mode

**Quiz mode**:
An optional per-Card Review style where the user answers a question about the Card before the reveal, then grades themselves as Forgot, Hard or Good.
_Avoid_: Test, exam

**Chat Review**:
A Review run by the user's AI assistant inside a conversation through the KIM connector, self-graded and counted like any other Review.
_Avoid_: Remote review, AI review

**Nudge**:
The push notification that invites the user to a Review, phrased as a question about the Card's Cue, or a Grouped Nudge.
_Avoid_: Alert, reminder notification

**Nudge window**:
A user-chosen time range, in the user's time zone, during which Nudges may be sent, subject to the Daily cap. It may run across midnight.
_Avoid_: Schedule, slot

**Daily cap**:
How many single-Card Nudges a user gets per local day, 3 by default.
_Avoid_: Limit, quota

**Grouped Nudge**:
The one Nudge a day that gathers the due Cards past the Daily cap ("4 more things to reinforce"), opening the run of due Cards. A due Card not nudged waits for the next Nudge window.
_Avoid_: Digest, summary

### Capture

**Capture**:
The act of condensing an AI assistant conversation into a single Card through the KIM connector, after the user confirms the draft in the conversation.
_Avoid_: Import, sync, export

**Captured Card**:
A Card created through Capture, the unit counted against the free plan limit.
_Avoid_: Import credit
